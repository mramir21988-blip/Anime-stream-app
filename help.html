Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to expand the answer.

Why is a video not playing?
Try switching to a different server using the server selector below the player. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or try a different browser.
How do I add something to Watch Later?
Click the bookmark icon on any movie or TV show card to save it to your Watch Later list.
Can I download movies or episodes?
THRILLING ANIME is a streaming platform and does not support downloads at this time.
Why do some episodes have no video?
Some newer episodes may not have been indexed by our servers yet. Try again later or report the issue using the Report Error button.
How do I change the video server?
On any watch page, you will find server options just below the video player. Click a different server to switch.
